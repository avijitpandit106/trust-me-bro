About

Trust Me Bro is a phishing detection platform designed to help users identify suspicious websites before interacting with them. The frontend provides an intuitive interface for entering URLs and viewing analysis results generated by the Flask backend.

Features

URL Analysis

Examine submitted URLs using phishing indicators generated by the backend.

Risk Scoring

Display a phishing risk score with clear classification levels.

Explainable Results

Understand why a website is considered safe, suspicious, or malicious through clear explanations.

Downloadable Incident Reports

Generate professional incident reports that can later be downloaded through the Flask backend.
